Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Coffee Bot is a personal automation tool that generates AI images and publishes them to a single Pinterest account owned by the developer.

Data Collection

This application does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to Pinterest users. It does not have public users or accept sign-ups.

Data Usage

The application uses the Pinterest API solely to publish content (images and descriptions) to the developer's own Pinterest board. No data from other Pinterest accounts is accessed, processed, or stored.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Coffee Bot is a personal automation tool that generates AI images and video clips and publishes them to social media accounts owned by the developer, including Pinterest, Tumblr, Bluesky, and YouTube.

Data Collection

This application does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to other users of these platforms. It does not have public users, accounts, or sign-ups of its own.

Data Usage

The application uses each platform's official API solely to publish content (images, video, and descriptions) to the developer's own accounts. No data belonging to other users or accounts is accessed, processed, or stored.

Google API Services

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
